## Exact limits and approvals

Arbitrary external host effects remain outside ECS rollback. A physically absent component is supported when its family is declared. Raw TS null services are present and reach the body before TypeError; actual World values roll back while attempt counters remain. Bend Incompatible slots are an explicitly stronger typed provisioning boundary, not a fabricated TS pre-execution refusal.
