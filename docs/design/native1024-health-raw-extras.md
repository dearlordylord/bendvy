# Conditional Health raw-extra storage probe

A private nominal Health slot with `levels:Array<U32>`, `rawExtras:Array<U32>` and six cached scalars has8 fields and may lower to class8 instead of the current9-field class16 slot. A persistent two-cell rawExtras owner may preserve rawreserve/rawclass at indices0/1 independently of cachedreserve/cachedclass. This is a conditional source representation design, not implementation or performance approval.

It is not a CP-only edit. Existing HA packed row types, taken/get/set/returned routes destruct the nominal9 fields and transport rawreserve/rawclass as scalars. They must carry the owned extras Array affinely across the whole row and preserve it during ordinary main writes. Allocating a new extras Array on every row-return would relocate costs and violate the intended persistent representation. All constructor/pattern/import frontiers must be inventoried before implementation; retaining an old wide slot at publication would not test the mechanism.

Original public Vitals/View headers and callback bodies remain unchanged. Ingress allocates exactly two cells once for a newly admitted slot; raw projection consumes/threads the extras owner through two reads and reconstructs the exact public Vitals. Cached get reads cached scalars without touching rawExtras. Main swap must return true-old levels values, retain every other levels cell and carry extras unchanged. Raw/cache reserve/class discrepancies remain observable. Ledger epoch/totals/cache and paired journals/rollback are unchanged. The generic arbitrary-Type route remains available.

If the new type is publicly constructible, short extras Arrays alias under modulo access. Require an actual size>=2 check before its optimized raw projection/row entry, or keep the new constructor confined behind a checked factory; an exported malformed payload cannot silently be treated as a valid two-scalar owner. Plain private naming alone is not confinement. No Data-only restriction, lossy scalar packing or levels-array size/content change is permitted.

Smallest gates: source-frontier byte/callback guards and checker; actual emitted slot/publication class8 and persistent extras transport; fresh Health complete65 plus allocation/word counts including extras ingress and raw projections; literal raw/cache discrepancy and all levels/extras cells, arrays of varying depths, true-old repeated writes, paired rollback, immutable retained Data, nonidentity returned owner and short-extras rejection/fallback controls. Existing concrete Health handoff nodes may also cross a class boundary once the owner layout changes; inspect rather than assume their class16 remains. Native word/call cost and timing remain open. This scope is broader than the proposed CP-only change and should not start without explicit bounded source ownership and time allocation.
