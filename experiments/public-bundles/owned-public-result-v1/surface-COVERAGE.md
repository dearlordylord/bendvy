## Existing #38/#46 policy boundaries

- Immediate reservation refusal: core Commands.spawn_reserved currently returns only World/Reservation and discards its generic payload on the rejected branch. Candidate Batch passes only Unit through that low-level route and retains the actual Packet outside it, so it can return that owner on refusal. Promote that separation; do not route an affine Packet directly through the legacy branch or redefine its cleanup policy here.
- Registered failure before activation: current Commands.tx_spawn explicitly retains reserved IDs on failure. The qualified surface observes the retained nextId/highWater/dead slot and recovered owners. Whether cancellation/failure retires, reuses or invalidates that ID remains #38; the observation does not approve a replacement policy.
- Delayed activation/population failure: core Commands.spawned suppresses activation rejection and returns World; Batch later validates the target and invokes typed Packet recovery. fallible-spawn offers explicit ActivationFailure/PopulationFailure callbacks, but choosing the resulting entity/reservation state and default recovery destination is still #38/#46. No failed activation was manufactured in the surface cohort.
- Pending cancellation is distinct from the qualified FIFO despawn-after-activation case. World.deactivate and a still-pending activate command do not by themselves define cancellation/tombstoning or owner recovery. Do not infer that policy from cleanup-after.
- Current World and Column bounds both admit IDs through 131072. Keep those source-current limits and existing pre-index validation; exhaustion/growth/refusal/retry and clock boundaries require #38's controls, not a new cap. Constructor-stage guarantees only its authored inverse. Canonical #46 construction now supplies checked retained-codec factories and owned requests (master64807397); canonical-constructor-head demonstrates that exact request through recursive Build.stage. No automatic inverse or external side-effect rollback is chosen.
