# Public resource-system delivery

`src/ecs/ordinary-resource-system.bend` is adopted on master. An ordinary resource
identity supplies the operational read/write grant and access declaration. A
registered resource-only body runs once per permitted invocation, including an
empty World; existing transaction rollback and registration checks apply.
Descriptions are derived explicitly from the actual Registry owner.

Six authority controls and complete normal/rollback-control JS/Native outputs
qualify the finite empty/two-entity consumer. Independent backend review is
recorded in `runtime-v1/launch-review.md`. The complete unchanged 112-module
Workshop gate and final review are retained in `regression-v1/actual-attempt01/`
and `regression-v1/launch-review.md`. All 118 monitored source files match the
integrated tree; the relocated consumer also passed stock Bend 2.0.35 source5.

The gate returned `NO_CONFIRMED_REGRESSION`. Paired median candidate/baseline
ratios were JS 0.860993 and Native 1.187760; Native was slower in 13/20 pairs,
with one-sided p=0.131588, above the unchanged Holm threshold 0.025. The observed
Native slowdown remains a limitation. Same-cohort JS/TS 0.394237 and Native/TS
0.099790 describe Workshop only; it does not call the new provider.

This provider replaces the entire resource owner `R`. Independent aggregate
fields/sibling confinement, automatic ResourceApp and schedule integration,
broader #56 coverage and feature-specific performance remain open. Raw
constructors are trusted; affine output discard retains the existing contract.
Historical preparation status and receipts remain unchanged.
