Current sources: `src/ecs/ordinary-system.bend`,
`ordinary-capture-query.bend`, `ordinary-app-run.bend`,
`ordinary-app-existing-execution.bend`, `system.bend`, `compose.bend`,
`transaction.bend`, `component.bend`, `commands.bend`, `structural.bend`.
Success-only tracked cursor update is in system.tracked_outcome; failure preserves
its existing registration. transaction.finish_world rolls back newest-first
inverses and publishes commands/events only on success. Component inverse
preserves old payload and lifecycle stamp. These source properties are not
universal proofs or fresh runtime receipts.

Reusable bounded evidence is described in `docs/reports/user-system-api.md`,
`docs/reports/public-query-contract.md`, and
`experiments/public-captures/ordinary-owner-v1/query-v1/FINAL-REVIEW.md`.
The latter accepts ordinary query failure/retry and affine recovery. Its App
fixture qualifies automatic metadata/owner folding but explicitly extracts
owners and calls F.run; it does not claim a general App scheduling executor.
Therefore these separate receipts alone do not establish automatic App execution
of captured query failure/retry. Later current consumers must be checked before
calling this an uncovered behavior.

The proposed smallest consumer registers one independently authored captured
query through the ordinary declarations, attaches its actual registered owner to
a public App execution recipe, and runs failure then same-owner retry through
Entry.run. It observes every capture cell, original instance/cursor, component
values/stamps, complete World/registration state and exact execution status.
It must not invoke gameplay directly from the outer driver. A second matching
row makes rollback nontrivial. Existing source-bound affine component/queue and
publication coverage remains separate unless the new consumer actually covers
it; the fixture does not gain those capabilities by a claim in this note.

No obsolete primitive adapter layout, five-second checker limit, expired trial
budget or special callback recognizer is selected. Typed authority, affine
ownership, rollback/order, identity, marks, publication and full observations
remain required. Final equivalent-work JS/TS<=1 and Native/TS<=0.5 and the full
five-family/three-size qualification remain under #21/#23/#24. No gate is waived,
no law/contract is changed, and no performance ratio follows from this package.
